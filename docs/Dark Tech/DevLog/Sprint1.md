# Sprint 1 - Design Process

## Sprint Overview

This was the very first sprint where we as a team had to start to get to know each other and had to begin with a direction for the project. During the Sprint we made the base concept of the provocatype we wanted to work on and did an individual analysis on a topic related to the project. For me that analysis was about uninhabitability and it can be found [here](../Analysis/Uninhabitability.md)

### Sprint Goals

For this sprint the other N3 and I decided to take the lead during the project and we discussed that we should start with a design. The reason for this was that we would have to make an installation as a final provocatype and therefore needed to start with a good design to use as the base of the idea. This would help with having something solid that could be expanded with extra small things to bring the project together.

So the goal for this Sprint was to have a base to work from and to brainstorm what we could bring together in Sprint 2.

### Planning

To get a better understanding of the project we needed to have a meeting with the stakeholder to get an understanding of the projects goal and what they wanted to achieve with it. I planned the meeting with the stakeholder and checked when they and the team had time. Luckily the meeting was able to be planned during the first half of the sprint.

After having a better understanding of what we needed to deliver the other N3 and me started with individual drawings so we could come together and pickout parts we loved from each drawing to then continue on to making a small prototype in 2 groups. But starting with individual drawings could result in to big of a difference in what needed to be done so we started with a brainstorm session and a mood board, so we would be more on the same line but still be able to have individual idea's.

## Research & Exploration

When we started we had no vision on what the project was about and what it had to accomplish but after talking with the stakeholder we got a better understanding. During the meeting the other N3 and me took the lead. We where the main speakers to get a better understanding for the project as we had more experience in doing so. In the meeting I made sure to keep track of the time as we only had about 15-20 minutes but had a lot of questions. To ensure we would have a decent understanding of the project i filtered out the questions we had made in advance to once that would achieve this result. In the end we did mis some questions that would help us understand the project a little bit more the we already had so I decided we should send those with the debriefing to the stakeholder to still get an answer on them, and we did.

### Initial Research

During the Brainstorm and mood board session i had a difficult time to come with ideas as I could not place my self in the correct mindset. It took me a bit to feel some creativity but the others came with ideas and this helped with me being able to feel more ideas coming up. During the brainstorm we came to a collective decision to start with a living room and have the experience within that room.

After the mood board we split up and went on to create individual ideas. But during that time we also had to do an individual analysis on a topic related to the project where mine was about uninhabitability. To read that analysis go to this [page](../Analysis/Uninhabitability.md).
